Puffy Pals - Privacy Policy

Developer: MBS Nova Studio · App: Puffy Pals (com.mbsnovastudio.puffypals) · Effective date: 28 November 2026 · Türkçe

Puffy Pals ("the game") is developed by MBS Nova Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, chest and daily goal), settings (sound, music, vibration, color symbols, language) and tutorial progress are saved on your device, together with the transaction IDs of your recent purchases (so each purchase is delivered once) and the end date of an active Puffy Pass subscription. We do not run our own servers and we do not create player accounts. If device backup is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud or computer backup on iPhone), this data may be included in your device backup, which Google or Apple stores for you (we cannot access it) and restores if you reinstall the game.

Age range. Where the law requires it, the game asks the app store for your age range (Google Play Age Signals on Android, Declared Age Range on iPhone); the store may ask you to share it first. It is used for one thing only: if the store says you are under 18, the game shows no ads at all and collects no analytics or crash reports (the ad, consent, analytics and crash reporting services are not turned on). The age range stays on your device. It is not sent to us, to Google AdMob or to any analytics, and it is not used to personalise or target ads. If you do not share it, the game works normally.

Feedback. Settings → Feedback opens your own email app with a message to info@mbsnovastudio.com. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself. Besides what you write, the prepared message contains the game version, device model, operating system and language, so we can reproduce bugs; you can delete these lines before sending. We also receive the email address you write from. Please do not include passwords or payment details.

3. Why we use this information

  • To show the optional rewarded ads that fund the free game (personalised only if you consent where consent is required).
  • To understand which levels are too hard or too easy and to fix crashes (analytics and crash reports, only with your consent where consent is required).
  • To deliver the coins, boosters and Puffy Pass benefits you buy, and to restore an active Puffy Pass after you reinstall.
  • To read and answer the feedback you send.

Legal bases where the GDPR applies: your consent for personalised ads, analytics and crash reports; performance of a contract when you buy something; your request when you send feedback. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices

  • Consent: where required, a consent form appears when you first play. Your choices there cover ads, analytics and crash reports, and you can change them at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Ads are optional: the game shows no ads unless you choose to watch one.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone the game does not use the IDFA.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the game removes the game data on your device; a copy in your device backup can be deleted in your Google or iCloud backup settings. To ask us to delete an email you sent, or analytics and crash data associated with your device, write to info@mbsnovastudio.com. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the retention period set for the project (we use the default or shorter) and are encrypted in transit.

Under the GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698, Article 11) you may contact us with any request or question at the address below; we answer within 30 days.

5. Permissions

Internet and network state (for ads, the app store, analytics and crash reports), advertising ID on Android (for ads; added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK together with Android's ad services permissions), billing (for in-app purchases through Google Play), wake lock and foreground service (added by Google's libraries for their background tasks), and vibration for short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. The game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

6. Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All ads in the game are limited to content suitable for all ages (rating G), and players the app store identifies as under 18 see no ads (see "Age range" above).

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date before that version is released.

8. Contact

  • Satın aldığınız coin, güçlendirici ve Puffy Pass avantajlarını vermek ve yeniden kurulumdan sonra etkin Puffy Pass'i geri yüklemek için.
  • Gönderdiğiniz geri bildirimi okuyup yanıtlamak için.

GDPR'nin uygulandığı yerlerde hukuki dayanaklar: kişiselleştirilmiş reklam, analitik ve çökme raporları için izniniz; bir şey satın aldığınızda sözleşmenin ifası; geri bildirim gönderdiğinizde talebiniz. Kişisel bilgilerinizi satmayız.

  • İzin: gerektiği yerlerde ilk oynayışta bir izin formu çıkar. Oradaki seçimleriniz reklam, analitik ve çökme raporlarını kapsar; istediğiniz zaman Ayarlar → Gizlilik seçenekleri'nden değiştirebilirsiniz.

Gönderdiğiniz bir e-postanın ya da cihazınızla ilişkili analitik ve çökme verilerinin silinmesini istemek için info@mbsnovastudio.com adresine yazın. Analitik ve çökme verileri Google tarafından proje için ayarlanan süre boyunca saklanır (varsayılanı ya da daha kısasını kullanırız) ve aktarım sırasında şifrelenir.

GDPR ve 6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu (KVKK, madde 11) kapsamında her türlü talep ve sorunuz için aşağıdaki adresten bize ulaşabilirsiniz; 30 gün içinde yanıtlarız.

Oyundaki tüm reklamlar her yaşa uygun içerikle (G derecesi) sınırlıdır ve uygulama mağazasının 18 yaşından küçük olarak bildirdiği oyunculara hiç reklam gösterilmez (yukarıda "Yaş aralığı").
