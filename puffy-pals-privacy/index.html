Puffy Pals - Privacy Policy

Developer: MBS Nova Studio · App: Puffy Pals (com.puffypals.game)

Effective date: 20 November 2026.

Puffy Pals ("the game") is developed by MBS Nova Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, chest and daily goal), settings (sound, music, vibration, color symbols, language) and tutorial progress are saved on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not create player accounts. If device backup is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud or computer backup on iPhone), this data may be included in your device backup, which Google or Apple stores for you (we cannot access it) and restores if you reinstall the game.

2. Information the game collects or shares

None. The game has no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no accounts. It does not collect, send or share personal information, it does not use an advertising identifier, and it works fully offline.

3. Permissions

The game uses the vibration permission for short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It requests no other permission.

4. Your choices

  • Vibration, sound and music can be turned off in Settings.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the game removes the game data on your device; a copy in your device backup can be deleted in your Google or iCloud backup settings.

5. Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if ads or statistics are added in a later version), the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date before that version is released.
